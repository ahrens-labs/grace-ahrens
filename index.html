Welcome

Hello, I'm Grace.

I strive to wield my words in a way that brings light to others. I have loved reading stories for as long as I can remember, especially stories with lots of magic and a few dragons. When I was ten, I decided that I wanted to write stories like the ones I have always loved reading. Stories that not only sweep readers away on a magical adventure, but touch their hearts and empower them on their great adventures through the real world.
