About

Grace Ahrens

Grace grew up on long fantasy series, annotated maps, and notebooks full of half-finished worlds. She writes fiction that balances wonder with consequence — stories where magic exists, but so do politics, grief, and hard choices.

When she is not writing, she reads folklore, collects story ideas from long walks, and drinks more tea than any reasonable person should admit to.

Her first novel is currently in progress. This site will be updated as publication news becomes available.
