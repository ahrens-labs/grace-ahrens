About

Grace Ahrens has spent years wandering the borderlands between waking life and the realms of imagination. A lifelong reader of epic fantasy, she writes stories where the old gods still whisper in the wind and every choice carries the weight of prophecy.

When she isn't drafting new adventures, Grace can be found collecting folklore, sketching maps of imaginary lands, and drinking far too much tea while debating whether dragons would prefer mountains or coastlines.

Her debut novel is currently in progress — stay tuned for news from the other side of the veil.
