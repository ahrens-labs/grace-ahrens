Have you ever wanted to write a book, but you weren't sure where to start? You may have story ideas, but you aren't sure if they are any good?

I'm here to help you.

I have been part of several writing programs, including the Young Writer's Workshop and the Young Writer's Honors Academy, and I have been studying writing seriously since I was fourteen. One thing I have learned is that as important as all the other elements of writing are, the most important element is your core story idea.

I want to help other writers make their story ideas as strong as possible to set them up for writing an incredible novel. That's why I created Blue Wing Editing. I offer a unique editing service where I give you feedback on your story concept, so that you can know that your story idea is as strong as it can be before you begin to draft.

I specialize in fantasy, but I may also branch out into editing other speculative fiction, mystery, action/adventure, or YA/middle-grade fiction in other genres if I believe the story is one I can work with well.

Do you have a story idea you want feedback on? Email grace@graceahrens.com to start the process of getting feedback.

The Process

Email me a brief summary of your idea at grace@graceahrens.com. The summary only has to be a few sentences. If you know what genre your idea falls into, let me know your genre. I will email you back to let you know whether I think your story concept would be a good fit for Blue Wing Editing, and if it is, I will also email you a template that you can fill out with more details on your story concept.

Once you finish filling out your story concept template, email it to me. For your first time using my editing service, I will let you know the strongest part of your concept and the part of your concept that needs the most work for free. I will also let you know the best method to pay me.

After you've paid for my editing service, you will receive a full feedback letter from me detailing areas of character, plot, theme, setting, and more. I will point out areas of your story concept that could be strengthened, advise you on how to strengthen those areas, and offer ideas to consider as you continue in the path of fleshing out your novel.
