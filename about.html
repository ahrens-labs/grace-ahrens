About Grace

I have loved reading for as long as I can remember. I love opening a book and being swept away on an adventure, more often than not one involving lots of magic and a few dragons. When I was ten, I decided that I wanted to write stories like the ones I have always loved reading. Stories that not only sweep readers away on a magical adventure, but touch their hearts and empower them on their great adventures through the real world.

When I'm not writing, I can usually be found pacing, drawing characters, burying my nose in a fantasy book, or filling my one-million-and-one notebooks with even more story ideas. I do not have any writing published yet, but if you would like updates on my writing, you are welcome to join my email list.
