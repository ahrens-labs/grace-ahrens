About Grace

I've loved fantasy for as long as I can remember — the kind of stories where characters face impossible choices and still try to do what's right.

I write epic fantasy about archives, empires, and the quiet courage it takes to question what everyone already believes. My fiction balances wonder with consequence: magic exists in my worlds, but so do politics, grief, and the cost of telling the truth.

When I'm not writing, I read folklore, collect story ideas on long walks, and drink far too much tea. I grew up on annotated maps, sprawling series, and notebooks full of half-finished worlds — and I'm still filling those notebooks today.

My debut novel is currently in progress. If you'd like updates as it moves toward publication, you're welcome on the mailing list.
